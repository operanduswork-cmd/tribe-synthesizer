        How to read the lanes

        Each lane is one predicted average-brain response line, smoothed over about 2 seconds. The middle line is this reel's own average for that line. Above it means higher than usual for this reel, below means lower. There are no units and nothing here is a rating.

        Peaks and flat stretches come from all four lines together, after removing the slow drift across the reel. They locate moments to look at. They are not yet tested against any real retention graph, so treat them as a second opinion.

        The model predicts an average brain, not your audience. One row is one second, already aligned by the model. Seconds with black frames are left out of peak finding, and the first and last 2 seconds are less reliable.

        Times are whole seconds as the clock shows them, and a range includes its last second: 0:12-0:14 means 0:12, 0:13 and 0:14.
